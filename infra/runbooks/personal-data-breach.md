# Personal-data breach and ICO notification assessment

**Trigger:** Evidence suggests unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration,
or loss of supporter, project, account, payment metadata, private content, or
recovery data.

**Contain:** Name an incident lead and privacy/legal owner. Revoke sessions,
tokens, signed URLs, and provider credentials in scope; isolate affected
objects and restrict logs and exports. Preserve audit records. Do not alter or
delete evidence to make the incident smaller.

**Evidence:** Establish affected systems, data categories, subjects, access
window, recipients, volume, containment time, and likely harm. Store evidence
in the restricted incident location with hashes and access history. Keep raw
PII out of chat and tickets.

**Communicate:** Notify the privacy/legal owner immediately for the ICO
notification assessment and required timing. Notify affected users and
projects when advised. Use the independent status path for a service impact,
without naming individuals.

**Recover:** Close the access path, rotate credentials, invalidate caches and
URLs, restore from a known-good backup when needed, and verify deletion or
containment requests. Record regulator and user communications, then review
retention, access policy, and monitoring.

**Owner:** Privacy/legal lead. Postmortem owner: operations lead.
